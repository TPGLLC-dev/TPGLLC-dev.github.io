What it does

Lightning Lane, live

Every ride shows whether its Lightning Lane is sold out or the next return time, plus when it usually sells out.

Downtime history

When a ride goes down, see how long its outages usually last before you decide to wait.

Best deals right now

The rides running shortest against their usual wait, so you know where to head next.

Party nights and park maps

Plan around Halloween and Christmas party tickets, and see which land is busiest on the park map.

How close are the forecasts?

Every ride's wait is forecast hour by hour, by models trained nightly on months of recorded waits and the day's weather. Each forecast is checked against the real wait the next morning.
