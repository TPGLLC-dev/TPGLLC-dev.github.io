What makes the Pulse apps different

A live wait tells you about now. The Pulse apps also tell you what's coming.

See what's coming

Hour-by-hour forecasts for every ride, learned from months of recorded waits and the day's weather.

Hear when it changes

Set the wait you'll accept and get an alert when it's there, or when a ride that broke down reopens.

A plan that keeps up

The planner builds your day around the forecasts and adjusts when a ride goes down. New planning ideas are replayed against real park days before they ship.
