BeachPulse

Which beach today, and why.

Tell it when you are free, how far you will drive and what matters to you. BeachPulse scores every beach in reach on live conditions and real driving time, then explains the trade-off you make by taking the winner.

Coming soon to iPhone Free to download. BeachPulse Pro is a one-time $4.99 purchase. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

Public beaches across Tampa Bay, Sarasota, the Space Coast and South Florida.

Live conditions

Air and feels-like temperature, cloud, rain, wind and UV, hour by hour across the exact window you are free.

Water, surf and tide

Water temperature, wave height and tide from NOAA stations, so swimmable is a reading, not a guess.

Time actually on the sand

Live traffic, parking and the walk from the car all come off your window before anything is called a good day.

It shows its work

Every pick names what beats the runner-up and what you give up. A reading it does not have says "not measured", never zero.
