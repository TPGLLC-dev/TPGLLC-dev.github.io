LinksPulse

Where to play golf, and why.

Tell it when you want to play, how many are in your group, where you are starting from and what you want to spend. LinksPulse ranks the courses for that day and explains why each one placed where it did.

Coming soon to iPhone Free to download. LinksPulse Pro is a one-time $6.99 purchase. No account, no sign-in, no ads.

What it does

Courses across Orlando, Kissimmee and the Disney area.

Weather for the whole round

All four hours, not just tee time, including when afternoon thunderstorms are likely, plus official weather alerts.

Drive time and green fees

Real drive time from your hotel and the green fee at the tee time you are considering, against your budget.

Know the course

Cart, dress code, rental clubs, practice areas, tees with ratings and slopes, and a hole-by-hole map for most courses.

Book with the course

LinksPulse does not sell tee times. The booking button opens the course’s own page, then it reminds you before your round.

Free and Pro

When two courses are too close to call, LinksPulse says so instead of pretending one is clearly better.

Free

No account, no sign-up

  • Today’s and tomorrow’s rankings, and the reasons behind them
  • Weather alerts for each course
  • Saved tee times, reminders and sharing with your group
  • Storm alerts for a saved round, from two days before through the last hole (you turn it on per round)

LinksPulse Pro

$6.99, once. No subscription.

  • Any of the next seven days
  • Your week at a glance, every day side by side
  • Plan your rounds: the best course each day, never the same one twice
  • Your booked rounds all week: forecast and leave-by time from the day you save them
  • Start from anywhere: your hotel, a street address or a town

Support and privacy

No account and no ads. Your saved rounds stay on your phone, and your starting point is rounded to about 100 meters before it leaves it.
